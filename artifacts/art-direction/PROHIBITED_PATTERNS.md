# Season of Broken Hearts — prohibited visual patterns

This list is the Stage 9.4 project-owned visual guardrail. It supplements
`ART_BIBLE.md` and `VISUAL_LANGUAGE.json`; it does not replace source-bound
briefs or actual-runtime visual acceptance.

- No identity drift, age regression, swapped speaker, duplicate actor or actor
  whose clothing contradicts the authored timeline.
- No invented readable documents, logos, watermarks, captions, interface text,
  dialogue or buttons inside the source artwork.
- No premature Aurea terms, verified contracts, branch-specific evidence or
  other knowledge that the authored cue has not established.
- No romantic embrace, kiss or accepted touch in a refusal/neutral state; the
  two public-gesture consent states remain separate compositions.
- No floating, sinking or anatomically malformed people; feet, hands, props and
  supporting surfaces must remain physically grounded.
- No accidental wide-angle distortion, enlarged heads/hands, clipped faces or
  dialogue-safe silhouettes hidden by the runtime overlay.
- No generic luxury-fantasy hotel, random hearts/petals/glow, symbolic jealousy
  props or decorative effects that compete with the authored action.
- No sprites introduced solely to reduce CG count, and no portrait crop,
  stretch, blurred fill or upscale substituted for an independently composed
  landscape image.
- No CSS mask, object-position tweak or repeated cosmetic regeneration used to
  conceal a confirmed source-art defect.

Acceptance remains fail-closed: source PNG evidence, current runtime pixels,
provenance and independent review are separate required proof surfaces.
