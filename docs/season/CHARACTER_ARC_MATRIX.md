# Character Arc Matrix

## Nika — protagonist and non-romantic spine

| Phase | Starting position | Pressure | Choice that changes her | Observable change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EP1 | Uses control to survive betrayal | New job and three men with unfinished business | asks for facts or protects a boundary | recognizes Villa crisis as her work, not someone else's drama |
| EP2–3 | Curious but guarded, professionally capable | wedding work, budget pressure, Bianca's business case, Leo's return | makes an operational decision, sets a public boundary, asks for proof or an action | distinguishes listening from forgiving and refusal from failure |
| EP4–5 | Can be publicly visible | wedding performance, staff cuts, Elio's offer | names consent and career limits | stops treating refusal as a loss of agency |
| EP6 | Informed, angry, capable of choice | hidden terms and triangulated loyalties | explicit route-lock | chooses a relationship shape or independence |
| EP7–8 | Tests chosen future | partner career and alternative budget | stays, negotiates or puts herself first | turns boundaries into repeatable actions |
| EP9–10 | Owns public narrative | truth, hotel result and last offer | final relationship/career decision | leaves summer with a self-authored next step |

## Villa staff and secondary roles

The staff are not a single romance meter. `staffTrust` tracks whether the team
believes Nika will tell the truth and protect their work; individual scenes
should still show different needs. The sister's wedding is an event with its
own emotional stakes, not a device to force Matteo and Nika together. The
former fiancé remains a bounded reference to Nika's past and never reclaims the
season's centre.

## Route differentiation test

Each route must pass all five checks before its episode is accepted:

1. Its partner initiates at least one consequential action outside romance.
2. Its signature conflict cannot be moved to another route by renaming.
3. A refusal changes the route's later scene, not only one reaction line.
4. The protagonist's career choice remains meaningful without the partner.
5. The ending resolves the route's emotional premise and names its cost.
